Bounce or Bust Privacy Policy

\

Last updated: November 13th, 2026

\

Bounce or Bust is made by Rainewood Studios. This policy explains what information the game uses.

\

Information the game keeps on your device

\

Your progress (best height, Sparks, purchases made with Sparks, achievements and settings) is saved only on your device. We don't have accounts, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts or files.

\

Ads

\

The game shows optional rewarded ads, provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), and information about how ads are viewed and how the app performs. Players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for consent first. You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's settings.

\

Children

\

Bounce or Bust is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

\

Changes

\

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
